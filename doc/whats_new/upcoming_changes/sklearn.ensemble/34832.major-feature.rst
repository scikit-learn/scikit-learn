- :class:`ensemble.RandomForestClassifier`, :class:`ensemble.RandomForestRegressor`,
  :class:`ensemble.ExtraTreesClassifier`, and :class:`ensemble.ExtraTreesRegressor`
  now accept the `categorical_features` parameter, defaulting to `"from_dtype"` like
  :class:`~sklearn.ensemble.HistGradientBoostingClassifier`.
  Random forests support binary classification and single-output regression with up to
  255 categories per feature. Extra trees support up to ``2**24 - 1`` categories per
  feature, including multi-class and multi-output targets.
  ``warm_start`` is not supported together with categorical features.
  By :user:`Adam Li <adam2392>`.
