- The Lloyd iterations of :class:`cluster.KMeans` and
  :class:`cluster.BisectingKMeans` scale better with the number of threads: the
  threads no longer wait for each other during an iteration, and their buffers
  are allocated once per fit instead of at each iteration. This makes them
  several times faster on machines with many cores, and on sparse data with
  many features.
  By :user:`Arthur Lacote <cakedev0>`.
