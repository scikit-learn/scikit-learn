- The checks for missing values (NaN) in the inner loops of :mod:`sklearn.tree` and
  of the tree-based estimators of :mod:`sklearn.ensemble` are now always inlined.
  This makes these estimators faster on builds where the `isnan` function of the
  C library was not inlined by the compiler, e.g. the Linux packages of conda-forge.
  The speed-up is most notable when fitting :class:`ensemble.ExtraTreesRegressor`
  and :class:`ensemble.ExtraTreesClassifier`.
  By :user:`Arthur Lacote <cakedev0>`.
