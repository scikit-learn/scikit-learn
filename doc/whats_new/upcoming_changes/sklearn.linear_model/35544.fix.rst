- The line search of the solver `"newton-cg"` of
  :class:`linear_model.LogisticRegression`, :class:`linear_model.GammaRegressor`,
  :class:`linear_model.PoissonRegressor` and :class:`linear_model.TweedieRegressor`
  now uses the approximate Wolfe conditions of
  :doi:`Hager & Zhang (2005) <10.1137/030601880>` to accept steps for which the change
  of the loss is of the order of machine precision. It replaces the check on the sum
  of absolute gradients.
  By :user:`Christian Lorentzen <lorentzenchr>`.
