- :func:`tree.export_dict` exports a fitted decision tree as a nested dict,
  including categorical splits, optionally writing it to `out_file` as JSON.
  :func:`tree.export_text`, :func:`tree.export_graphviz`, and :func:`tree.plot_tree`
  also display categorical splits with their original labels and indicate
  missing-value and unknown-category routing. By :user:`lcrmorin`.
