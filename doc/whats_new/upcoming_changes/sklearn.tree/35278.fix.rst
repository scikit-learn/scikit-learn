:func:`sklearn.tree.DecisionTreeClassifier` and
:func:`sklearn.tree.DecisionTreeRegressor`: fixed the ``categorical_features``
docstring and the validation error message.

- The docstring now correctly states the actual category limits: at most 256
  unique categories with ``splitter='best'`` and at most 16,777,216 with
  ``splitter='random'`` (previously stated 255, which was wrong).
- The ``ValueError`` raised when a feature exceeds the limit now reports the
  number of categories found and the actual limit for the chosen splitter,
  instead of referring to an internal encoded value range.

:pr:`35278` by :user:`MilindLate`.
