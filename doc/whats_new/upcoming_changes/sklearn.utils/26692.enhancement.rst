- :func:`utils.check_array` and :func:`utils.check_X_y` now accept complex data
  when `dtype` is, or contains, a complex type such as `np.complex128`. This
  lets third-party estimators opt in to complex inputs and targets through
  :func:`utils.validation.validate_data`. The default `dtype="numeric"` still
  rejects complex data.
  By :user:`Miles Cranmer <MilesCranmer>`.
