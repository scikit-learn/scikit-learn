- :class:`preprocessing.TargetEncoder` now accepts `sample_weight` in
  :meth:`~preprocessing.TargetEncoder.fit` and
  :meth:`~preprocessing.TargetEncoder.fit_transform`. Category encodings, the target
  mean and, with `smooth="auto"`, the target variances are then computed as weighted
  statistics, such that integer weights are equivalent to repeating samples.

  :user:`Bhavek Jamnadas <bhavek-jamnadas>`.
